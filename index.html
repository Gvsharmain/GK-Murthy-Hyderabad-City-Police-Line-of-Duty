01 · BACKGROUND

The record in one view

01

Service

Official records in the case file identify Late Sri G. Krishna Murthy as a Sub-Inspector of Police. A 1985 promotion order records his posting as an S.I. in the C.C.B. The family’s later records identify service with the Special Investigation Team (SIT).

02

Death

He died on 03 February 2001 while in Government service. The family seeks examination of the exact duty circumstances and the applicable Line of Duty / Duty Nexus framework.

03

Family proceedings

Smt. G. Bhavani submitted the original compassionate appointment application on 19 March 2001. The parent department subsequently verified and forwarded the proposal to the District Collector because no suitable vacancy was available in Hyderabad City Police.

Documentary distinction: the compassionate-appointment question and the Line of Duty / Police Martyr question are separate administrative issues. The family’s LoD representations specifically request examination under the LoD / Duty Nexus framework independently of Police Martyr policy.
02 · COMPASSIONATE APPOINTMENT

2001 → 2022 → continuing representations

The available records show a prolonged sequence of administrative processing rather than a single application followed by a single decision.

19 MAR 2001

Application initiated

Smt. G. Bhavani applied for compassionate appointment, seeking Junior Assistant. The later factual report records that eligibility documents, dependency, legal heirship, character and educational qualification were examined.

10 DEC 2002

Parent department forwards proposal

The Commissioner of Police forwarded the verified proposal to the District Collector, citing absence of a suitable OC vacancy in Hyderabad City Police.

18 MAR 2003

Nursing Orderly appointment

The Collector issued proceedings appointing Smt. G. Bhavani as Nursing Orderly. Subsequent representations sought a suitable alternative post.

2004

Alternative-post representations

The record includes representations for a suitable post and a Nizamia General Hospital communication stating that joining could not be permitted because no OC Nursing Orderly vacancy was available.

17 OCT 2005

Transition to Junior Assistant

Collectorate Proceedings No. A4/6169/2004 cancelled the earlier Nursing Orderly appointment and initiated consideration for Junior Assistant.

2006–2007

Qualification, option and vacancy process

The file records conditional Junior Assistant consideration, educational qualification developments, Local/Non-Local examination, willingness to remain under Hyderabad District, further verification and family affidavits.

23 AUG 2017

Dependent-son representation

Sri G. Vishnu Sharma submitted a representation after attaining majority seeking compassionate appointment in continuation of the unresolved historical proceedings.

02 NOV 2017

Endorsement

The Collector’s endorsement declined the request at that stage, primarily referring to the prescribed upper age limit and the then-stated absence of a provision for appointment of the dependent son.

16 MAR 2021

Detailed factual report

The District Collector reconstructed the complete history under File A4/6169/2004, including the original application, Nursing Orderly stage, Junior Assistant proceedings and later representations.

09 MAY 2022

Government decision

Government Memo No. 434-P/Ser.II/A2/2020-2 communicated that the compassionate appointment request could not be considered at that stage, principally having regard to the long lapse of time.

11 FEB 2026

Further Government/Police examination

The DGP memorandum cited in the current records forwarded the representation of Smt. G. Bhavani and Sri G. Vishnu Sharma for examination and necessary action as per rules in force.

Important: The website does not declare that compassionate appointment is legally established as an entitlement today. It records what the documents show and identifies the issues the family asks the competent authority to examine.
03 · LINE OF DUTY

A separate question from Police Martyr status

The family’s position

The 17 October 2025 representation sought re-classification from “expired while in service (natural cause)” to Death in the Line of Duty / Police Martyr status and raised a Duty Nexus argument.

After the DGP response of 15 December 2025 and the Commissioner’s endorsement of 19 January 2026, the 21 February 2026 representation expressly requested administrative review under the LoD framework and Duty Nexus test, independent of Police Martyr policy.

04 March 2026 — official forwarding

The Hyderabad City Police communication No. HCP/W/W3/0004/2025-26 states that the 21 February 2026 application concerning LoD classification was forwarded to the Director General of Police, Telangana, for further necessary action.

DOCUMENTED DUTY RECORD

A 2001 Police order records that S.I. G. Krishna Murthy, attached to Rakshak Mobile (N/Z) under SIT, had expired on 03-02-2001 and his name was struck off the City Police rolls w.e.f. 04-02-2001.
